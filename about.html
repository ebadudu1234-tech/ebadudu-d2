Let Me Guide You

Zihe Wang

  • VISUAL COMMUNICATION
  • INFORMATION DESIGN

01 / 04

I find my way by landmarks.

After walking a route once, I remember its buildings and details almost like a photograph, and the next time I know exactly where to go. I think this is my special skill. When I travel and someone takes the wrong turn, I just say, "Follow me, I know where to go."

02 / 04

Long text can be overwhelming, but one clear path tells you where you are. To me, design should guide people.

I work in visual communication and information design, and I create visuals that act as landmarks, so people can find what they need at a glance.

03 / 04

I design for everyday people, like the community members I registered as a hospital volunteer during COVID. With teammates, I keep everything clear and organized. On my first project as a team leader, I was strong and strict, and it caused conflicts with my team.

I learned that guiding people also means seeing what each person is good at, and encouraging them.

04 / 04

When a path is blocked, I do not give up.

I like a twist on a Chinese saying: "Nothing is hard if you are willing to give up." But I never give up the goal, only the route that is not working. In my free time, I go on road trips, and every trip reminds me how big the world is and how many routes are still waiting for me to explore.

Let me guide you.
